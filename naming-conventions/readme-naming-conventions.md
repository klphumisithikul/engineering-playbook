## General Rule of Thumb
Prefer **descriptive over clever**, **consistent over "technically more correct,"** and **lowercase-with-separators over mixed case** for anything that will be typed in a shell or URL (files, branches, cloud resources) — case sensitivity differences between macOS/Windows/Linux filesystems are a recurring source of real bugs.
